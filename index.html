FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the area, the surface type and how much build-up there is. Send over the rough dimensions and a photo and we'll come back with a firm price — quotes are always free and there's no obligation.

How long will it take?

Most domestic driveways and patios are a single day's work. Resanding and sealing can add time, since the surface needs to dry properly first. We'll tell you what to expect when we quote.

Do you need my water and electricity?

An outside tap and a standard socket are ideal, and we'll always ask first. Let us know when you get in touch if access is difficult and we'll work around it.

Will pressure washing damage my block paving?

Not when it's done correctly. The risk comes from too much pressure held too close, which erodes the surface and blasts out the jointing sand. We match the pressure and nozzle to the surface, and resanding afterwards puts back what the wash takes out.

What is resanding and sealing, and do I need it?

Resanding refills the joints between blocks with kiln-dried sand, which keeps the paving stable and slows weed growth. Sealing adds a protective layer on top that helps resist staining and keeps it looking clean for longer. Neither is essential, but together they make the results last considerably longer.

How often should it be done?

For most West London driveways, every one to two years keeps things looking sharp. Shaded or north-facing areas green up faster and may want doing annually. Sealed surfaces tend to go longer between cleans.
